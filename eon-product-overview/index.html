Outcomes

Get the job, and secure it

Three products that carry capability into work, income and a venture of your own.

1

Career Compass

A guided path from the skills a person can show to roles they can hold, with Brainy beside them.

Career Compass

Skills to jobs to income.

Career Compass is how the campus answers a changing labour market. It does not hand someone a placement. It shows a personal route: what they can already do, which roles still need people, and the learning that closes the gap. Pathways are mapped to real work across the campus industry segments. Brainy, the photorealistic mentor, stays with the learner through the steps, from the first look at a role to the evidence they can take to an employer.

The path is built as a sequence, not a catalogue to browse and forget. A learner can see the next step, the skill it depends on, and how that skill connects to paid work. That is what “secure it” means here: a route they can return to when the job changes, not a single recommendation.

Open on the campus ↗
2

Venture Builder

About ninety days from a local problem to something that can be launched, without a technical background.

Venture Builder

Turn what you have learned into a venture.

Venture Builder is for the person who can see a problem in their own place and does not yet have a way to build the answer. AI coding agents and solution templates do the technical work with them. The aim is a real offer, not a slide deck. The campus describes a path of about ninety days from idea to launch.

What leaves with them is something they can show and, when it is ready, list. The EON Marketplace is the place institutions already come to look for tools and courses. Venture Builder is the step that turns a trained person into someone who can create income for others, not only hold a job.

Open on the campus ↗
3

AI Fluency

One hundred courses in five groups. The point is to use AI in real work, not to hear a lecture about it.

AI Fluency

The skill under every other skill.

AI Fluency uses the same learn, train and perform shape as the rest of the campus. Brainy asks questions instead of reading a script. Practice is a real situation: a negotiation, a decision, a piece of work someone could actually be asked to do. Each group ends with evidence, including an EON AI Fluency Index score and work the learner can show.

The proficiency ladder runs from someone who only uses AI as a search box, through people who automate tasks and collaborate with it, to someone who builds systems other people rely on. Five groups cover that range. The programme is cross-cutting: it sits under Career Compass and Venture Builder, because both of those outcomes depend on a person who can work with AI rather than only talk about it.
