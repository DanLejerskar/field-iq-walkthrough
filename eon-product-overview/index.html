Outcomes, in more detail

Learn, Train, and Perform open the longer pages on the education review. These four stay here.

SPATIAL

Career Compass

Skills to jobs to income.

Career Compass shows a personal route: what a person can already do, which roles still need people, and the learning that closes the gap. It does not hand someone a placement. Brainy stays with the learner from the first look at a role to the evidence they can take to an employer.

The path is a sequence they can return to when the job changes. That is what it means to secure the work, not a single recommendation.

SPATIAL

Venture Builder

Turn what you have learned into a venture.

Venture Builder is for someone who can see a problem in their own place and does not yet have a way to build the answer. AI coding agents and solution templates do the technical work with them. The campus describes about ninety days from idea to launch.

What leaves with them is something they can show and, when it is ready, list where institutions already look for tools and courses.

SPATIAL

AI Fluency

The skill under every other skill.

One hundred courses in five groups. Brainy asks questions instead of reading a script. Practice is a real situation, and each group ends with evidence, including an EON AI Fluency Index score and work the learner can show.

The ladder runs from someone who only uses AI as a search box to someone who builds systems other people rely on. Career Compass and Venture Builder both depend on that skill.

SPATIAL

Sentient Worlds

Replace scrolling with exploring.

Announced on 17 March 2026. The Wonder Engine turns a topic into a cinematic experience. The examples in the announcement are a volcano, Mars, and the human heart.

The Progressive Immersion Ladder has four steps: a 60-second film, a conversation with Brainy, a 3D place you can walk through, and the same place brought into the room with a phone. Oh-Wow Explore is the personal path, including stepping into a job. Oh-Wow Learn is the version for a school or a company.
